Why This Matters

What happens when
they come for you

  • 01.

    You Receive a Demand Letter

    A law firm identifies your store has WCAG violations. You get a letter demanding settlement before they file. Most brands panic and pay — $5,000 to $20,000 — just to make it go away.

  • 02.

    You Get Served

    If you don't respond or dispute, they file in federal court. New York, Florida, and California are the most active jurisdictions. Illinois saw a 745% spike in 2025.

  • 03.

    Legal Fees Stack Up

    Even winning costs money. Defense attorney fees, court costs, and lost time responding to discovery requests add up fast — often exceeding the settlement they originally demanded.

  • 04.

    You Get Targeted Again

    Serial plaintiff firms track prior defendants. If you settled once without fixing the underlying code, you're on a list. Nearly half of 2024 defendants had been sued before.

⚠ The Hard Truth
"Installing a widget doesn't make you compliant. It makes you a better target."

In 2024, the FTC fined accessiBe $1M for false advertising. Their widget — and dozens like it — promised compliance but delivered lawsuits instead. 456 brands with widgets installed were still sued in the first half of 2025 alone.

The only defence that holds up in court is code-level remediation — fixing the actual HTML, ARIA attributes, keyboard navigation, and color contrast at the source.

⚑  Apparel and restaurant brands make up 60% of all filings. If that's your category — you're the primary target.
