    About

    I'm a senior software engineer at PagePilot AI, and I've spent 5+ years building (and breaking) things in the Shopify ecosystem.

    I build tools that take work off merchants' plates: less headache, more sales, less stress. AltMaster is one of them, a free app that writes alt text for product images so merchants don't have to.

    PagePilot has 190+ reviews, and running at that scale teaches you things the docs don't. Here I share the stories behind what we build and break, from an engineering point of view.
